Leakage-Free Chronological Temporal Holdout Protocol
event_repeat Zero Data Leakage Protocol (75% Train / 25% Test Split)

For each evaluated customer, interactions are ordered strictly chronologically by event_timestamp ASC. The earliest 75% of events are used exclusively as pre-cutoff training history to build profile vectors and the collaborative matrix. The final 25% of subsequent events are held out strictly as future ground truth. Post-cutoff events, orders, or interactions are strictly prohibited from entering profile construction.

info RecSys Academic Sparsity Caveat

In an uncurated production e-commerce catalog of 34,190 products, exact item-level hits are naturally sparse (< 10%) because users only interact with a minuscule fraction of products. Combining exact item-level holdout metrics (Precision@K, Recall@K, NDCG@K) with Category Alignment, Intra-List Diversity (ILD), and Catalog Coverage provides a rigorous, academically defensible RecSys benchmark.
